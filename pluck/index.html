Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 17, 2026

Pluck helps you turn photos into stickers. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Photos and Camera

Pluck can access the camera and the photos you choose so it can create stickers. Your photos are used to perform the sticker-making features you request.

Sticker Creation

Some background-removal features may send the selected image to a processing service so the cutout can be generated. Images are used for that requested processing flow.

Purchases

Pluck may offer premium features through Apple in-app purchases. Apple processes purchase and subscription transactions.

Advertising

Pluck may show ads. Ad networks may process device or ad-related identifiers according to the choices you make in iOS privacy prompts.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require an account to use Pluck.
  • We do not sell your personal photos.
  • We do not ask for your contacts, precise location, or health data.

Children

Pluck is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can help.

Changes

We may update this policy as Pluck changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
