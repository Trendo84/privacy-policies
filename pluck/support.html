Common Help

  • If camera access is blocked, open iOS Settings, find Pluck, and enable Camera access.
  • If a photo cannot be imported, confirm Photos access is enabled for selected photos or all photos.
  • If stickers do not appear in Messages, restart Messages and check that Pluck is enabled in the Messages app drawer.
  • If a premium purchase does not unlock, use Restore Purchases in the app and confirm the Apple ID used for the purchase.
