Common Help

  • If camera capture is unavailable, check Camera permission in iOS Settings and restart Patina.
  • If importing or saving fails, check Photos permission and available device storage.
  • If video export is slow, keep Patina open until export finishes and avoid Low Power Mode for large videos.
  • If Memory Capsule reminders do not appear, check Notification permission for Patina.
  • If Patina AI does not respond, confirm you have network access and an active Patina Pro entitlement.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are managed through Apple. You can cancel or manage subscriptions in App Store account settings.
