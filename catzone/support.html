Common Help

  • If stickers do not appear in Messages, restart Messages and check that the sticker app is enabled in the Messages app drawer.
  • If animated stickers do not animate, confirm your device is running a current iOS version and try reopening the conversation.
  • If a purchase or download issue occurs, check your App Store account and purchase history. Apple handles App Store purchases and refunds.
  • If the app will not install or update, restart the device and confirm there is enough free storage.
