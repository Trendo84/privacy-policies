Miki FM Accessibility

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Declared App Store Accessibility Support

Miki FM declares support for Dark Interface on supported Apple platforms. The app is designed around a dark radio-console interface and includes selectable visual themes.

Supported Features

  • Dark Interface: Miki FM uses a dark, high-atmosphere audio-console design by default and provides theme choices in the app.
  • Readable controls: Core playback controls use large tap targets and clear labels such as play, pause, previous, next, random, search, and settings.
  • Apple platform controls: The app uses Apple platform UI where possible, including standard permission prompts for Apple Music, microphone access, tracking permission, and purchases.

Known Limitations

Miki FM is a visual radio app with custom meters, spectrum views, vinyl-style artwork, and station cards. Some decorative or visualizer-heavy areas may not fully communicate every visual detail through assistive technologies.

Live internet radio streams usually do not provide captions or audio descriptions. Because of that, Miki FM does not claim Captions or Audio Descriptions support for live station audio.

Tips

  • Use system volume controls or Control Center if you prefer platform-level audio control.
  • Use the in-app search to find stations by genre, city, or station name.
  • Use the app's theme settings if you prefer a different visual treatment.

Feedback

If an accessibility issue blocks a common task such as browsing stations, playback, search, settings, or premium purchase restore, please contact support with your device model and iOS, iPadOS, or tvOS version.
